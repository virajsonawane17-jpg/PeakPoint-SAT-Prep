Questions, answered

Is PeakPoint Prep free?

The current tools are free to use. You do not need to enter payment details.

Does PeakPoint predict or guarantee my SAT score?

No. Practice accuracy describes the questions you have answered. Use official practice tests for standardized score information.

Is AI tutoring available?

PeakPal is a clearly labeled interface preview. It does not currently provide AI responses. PeakLabs generation is also in development.

Where is my progress stored?

Authentication and some learning records use Supabase. Question-bank and vocabulary progress are stored in this browser under your account. Clearing browser storage can remove those records. See the privacy draft for details.

Is this affiliated with College Board?

No. PeakPoint Prep is independent. Question-bank content is attributed to College Board; no endorsement is implied.

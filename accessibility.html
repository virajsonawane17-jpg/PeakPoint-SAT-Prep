Accessibility Information

We aim to make SAT practice easier to use with a keyboard, readable layouts and clear feedback. Public pages include a skip link, visible focus indicators, responsive layouts, native FAQ controls and reduced-motion support.

The full app has not undergone an independent accessibility audit. Complex question content, MathML, modal focus, charts and all assistive-technology combinations require further testing. We do not claim WCAG conformance.

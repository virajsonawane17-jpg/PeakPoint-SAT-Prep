Privacy Policy

Date: July 8, 2026. This describes practices visible in the application code. Provider settings, operational procedures and legal requirements still require confirmation by the operator. This is not a claim of legal compliance.

Information used

Registration requests a display name, email and password. Supabase Auth handles credentials and sessions. Study tools may record goals, test dates, answers, correctness, sessions, vocabulary status, saved-question flags and learning settings. Do not use your full legal name if it is unnecessary.

Where records are stored

Supabase provides authentication and PostgreSQL storage for profiles, attempts, sessions, snapshots and learning state. The browser stores session tokens, account-specific question-bank and vocabulary progress, activity logs, planner inputs and a learning-state fallback. These browser records do not reliably synchronize across devices.

Third parties and requests

The app sends authentication and database requests to Supabase. Some pages request fonts from Google Fonts; linked resources may open third-party sites with their own policies. The deployed hosting provider, server access logs, provider region, backups and international transfers require operator verification.

Analytics and AI

No third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDK was identified in the reviewed source. Learning analytics are calculated from study records. PeakPal is a local interface prototype; no AI provider or AI request was identified. Reassess this policy before connecting an AI service.

Cookies and browser storage

The application uses browser local storage for authentication and learning functionality. No app-set tracking cookies were identified. Hosting or provider cookies have not been independently verified.

Retention and deletion

No automatic retention schedule was found in the code. Provider backup retention and operational deletion timelines remain unverified. You can clear this account's local study records in Settings. This does not delete server records or your account. See Account Deletion to request deletion.

Students and age safeguards

The service is intended for SAT preparation. A verified parental consent process is not implemented. The operator must review the actual audience, age-related obligations and data practices before accepting accounts where parental consent is required. Please contact us with child-data concerns; do not send additional sensitive information.

Questions and requests

Email virajsonawane17@gmail.com to ask about your data or request access, correction or deletion. Processing procedures and response timelines need operator confirmation.

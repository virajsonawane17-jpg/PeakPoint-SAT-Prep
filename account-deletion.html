Account Deletion

Request server-side deletion

Email peakpointprep@gmail.com from the account email with the subject “PeakPoint account deletion request”. Ask to delete your authentication account and related study records. Never include your password.

Deletion is handled by the operator; there is currently no automated account deletion endpoint. Identity verification, backup retention and completion timelines need operator confirmation. Sending an email does not mean deletion is complete.

Remove records on this device

Open Settings to clear this account's local learning records. This removes browser practice and vocabulary progress, planner settings and learning fallback records. It does not delete Supabase data, and server learning state can be downloaded again.

On shared devices, sign out when finished. Old records created before account isolation may remain in browser storage without an identified owner; clearing this site's browser storage removes them and signs you out.

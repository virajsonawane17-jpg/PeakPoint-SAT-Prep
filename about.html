About PeakPoint Prep

Making SAT Preparation More Accessible

PeakPoint Prep is a free SAT preparation platform developed to bring practice, study tools and performance insights into one accessible experience.

Why PeakPoint Prep Exists

SAT preparation can involve navigating multiple resources, identifying weaknesses and understanding difficult concepts. PeakPoint Prep brings these activities together, helping students approach preparation in a more organized and personalized way.

Built by a Student, for Students

PeakPoint Prep is an independently developed educational technology project founded by Viraj, a high school student interested in software engineering, artificial intelligence and accessible education. The project combines application development, educational content and ongoing technical improvements.

The application uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript with Supabase authentication and learning-record storage. Practice content and third-party services are attributed separately from the project's application work.

An Evolving Learning Platform

PeakPoint Prep continues to improve through feature development, testing, content updates and refinements to the learning experience. Current priorities include reliable progress records, accessible interactions and a working tutoring integration.
